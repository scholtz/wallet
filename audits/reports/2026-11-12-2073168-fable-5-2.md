## 1. Executive summary

This audit covers `f8a74a0..2073168` plus a fresh static review of all nine in-scope areas. No defect was found in key generation, derivation, encryption at rest or in the bytes that are signed. The eleven remediations of the previous report were re-traced: **AW-2026-100, -102, -103, -105, -106 and -108 are confirmed Closed**; **AW-2026-101, -104 and -107 are moved back from Closed to Mitigated** because each fix leaves a path uncovered; -109 and -110 stay Mitigated.

The pass found **0 Critical, 1 High, 2 Medium, 9 Low and 1 Informational new findings**. The High and one Medium are, once more, in the "what the user sees before clicking Sign" class, on the wallet's own `/sign` page:

- **AW-2026-112 (High):** the multisig review on `/sign` never shows the amount, the asset, an asset-transfer receiver or an application id. The rows read fields (`amount`, `name`, `tag`) that algosdk v3 transactions do not have. A multisig co-signer who pastes a proposal approves a transfer whose size and asset are not displayed. Present since the algosdk v3 upgrade (PR #134, 2026-01-04).
- **AW-2026-113 (Medium):** the same page labels every key registration "Tx to unregister from the participation" — online and non-participation registrations included — and shows no type-specific fields for `keyreg`, `acfg` or `afrz`. `/payWC` (this page) is the only signing screen for dApp requests whose sender is a multisig account.
- **AW-2026-114 (Medium):** the ARC-56 registry container is served under the wallet's own origin (`/arc56-registry/*`) from a floating `:latest` image that every deploy re-pulls. Whether that image can serve an active document (HTML/SVG) is not verifiable from this repository; if it can, it runs with the wallet's origin.

The Low findings: the new dApp fee ceiling is skipped for `signers: []` entries that the wallet still signs (confirmed with a probe); cached signatures survive failed or transport-dropped requests; asset transfers are labelled "Algo" when asset metadata is missing; deleting the last account is never persisted; account deletion needs no password and can orphan HD children; the Pay QR scanner ignores `network=`; a crafted gateway link shows "paid" and auto-forwards to any https site; application `access`/`rejectVersion` are signed but not shown; and several library-built flows skip the node-parameter checks.

Verification at HEAD: unit 435/435, lint, locale check and production build clean; 53 Playwright E2E tests on the production bundle pass (one needed a rerun after a trace-file write error, no assertion failed). `pnpm audit --prod` rose from 11 to **13 advisories (4 high, 5 moderate, 4 low)** with no dependency change — two newly published advisories.

After this audit the risk registry holds 124 rows: **29 Open**, **38 Mitigated**, **49 Closed** and **8 Accepted**.

## 2. Scope & methodology

Per `audits/ai-audit-instructions.md` @ `e0b7178`, sections 0–5. The orchestrator read the full `f8a74a0..2073168` source diff. Four fresh reviewer subagents each read one area in full (file lists in section 7) and returned candidate findings with file:line evidence plus a status for every carried-forward registry row in their area. The orchestrator then re-read the decisive lines of every rated finding (AW-2026-112..123) and of the three reopened rows before rating; items in the Informational bundle (AW-2026-124) and the unchanged carried-forward rows rest on the reviewers' traces and are marked as such.

Executed checks: a temporary unit spec against `admitTransactions` (removed afterwards) for AW-2026-115; a reviewer's `node -e` probe against `node_modules/algosdk` 3.9.0 confirming that a decoded transaction has no top-level `amount`/`voteFirst`; a reviewer's run of the `check-locales` regex and the installed vue-i18n compiler on bypass candidates; a reviewer's run of `@spliterati/shamir` below threshold; `pnpm audit`; the test suites listed in section 4.

Not exercised live: WalletConnect, Liquid Auth, Ledger, swaps, the scheduler and the multisig co-signing screen (no fixtures); their review is static. Out of scope as always: Algorand consensus, node/indexer software, third-party dApp code, and the content of container images not built from this repository.

## 3. Findings

### AW-2026-112 — Multisig review on `/sign` shows no amount, asset, asset-transfer receiver or application id — **High** — New

- **Affected:** `src/pages/Sign.vue:270-407` (multisig details block): `:291` amount row `v-if="multisigTxn?.amount"`, `:283-289` `multisigTxn?.name`, `:360-366` `multisigTxn?.tag`, `:393-402` receiver only for `multisigTxn?.payment?.receiver`; `:798-806` (`LooseTransaction` still declares `amount`, `name`, `tag`, `firstRound`, `voteFirst`, so `vue-tsc` accepts the bindings); `:32` (the plain review block that does show the amount is `v-if="!multisigTxn"`). Entry points: `src/pages/Pay.vue:1302-1326` (co-signer pastes a proposal), `Sign.vue:1365-1393` (own proposal), the `/payWC` multisig return.
- **Description:** algosdk v3 keeps the amount under `txn.payment.amount` / `txn.assetTransfer.amount`; there is no top-level `amount` (probe: `"amount" in d.txn === false`, `d.txn.payment.amount === 5000000n`). The amount row therefore never renders. For `pay` the co-signer sees type, fee, rounds, genesis, note, sender, receiver, rekey and close-to — not the amount. For `axfer` there is also no receiver and no asset id; for `appl` no application id.
- **Attacker precondition:** a multisig account, and a malicious or compromised co-signer/proposer, or a proposal blob altered in transit (proposals travel by chat or e-mail). The victim signs in the normal co-signing flow.
- **Impact:** independent review by each signer is the security property of a multisig account. A co-signer approves a transfer of arbitrary size, or of a different asset, that is not on the screen. Signing itself is correct; this is a display failure. Same defect class as AW-2026-007 and -100, whose fix (PR #222) corrected the rekey and sender rows of this block and left these.
- **Recommendation:** bind the rows to `payment?.amount ?? assetTransfer?.amount`, `assetTransfer?.receiver`, `assetTransfer?.assetIndex` and `applicationCall?.appIndex`; delete the dead fields from `LooseTransaction` so the type-checker catches the next rename; add an E2E that pastes a multisig proposal and asserts the amount.

### AW-2026-113 — `/sign` and `/payWC` label every key registration "unregister" and show no fields for `keyreg`, `acfg`, `afrz` — **Medium** — New

- **Affected:** `src/pages/Sign.vue:92-123` (`v-if="txn?.type == 'keyreg' && !txn.voteFirst"` → "Tx to unregister from the participation"; `voteFirst`/`voteLast` live under `txn.keyreg` in algosdk v3, `node_modules/algosdk/dist/types/transaction.d.ts:36`); `:51-60` (only `txn.type` for other kinds); no occurrence of `keyreg.`, `assetConfig`, `assetFreeze` or `assetSender` in the file. Entry points: `src/components/ConnectRequestsTable.vue:1339-1350` (multisig or rekeyed-to-multisig sender → `/payWC`), `src/pages/AccountOverview.vue:732-775` (the wallet's own online registration → `/sign`).
- **Description:** the "unregister" row is shown for every key registration and the round rows never render. The detail rows added to the request table for AW-2026-081/-082/-105 (role handover, destroy, freeze, clawback, participation keys) do not exist on this page.
- **Attacker precondition:** a dApp session for a multisig-controlled account ("Sign all" on the collapsed row goes straight to `/payWC`), or a hostile participation host / clipboard content in the wallet's own registration flow.
- **Impact:** an online registration with someone else's keys, or an irreversible `nonParticipation` registration, is reviewed as an "unregister"; an asset manager/clawback handover or a freeze is reviewed as a bare type label. No direct transfer of funds.
- **Recommendation:** reuse `describeKeyReg`, `describeAssetConfig`, `summarizeSpecialTransaction` and `DirectAppCard` from the request table in `Sign.vue`; read `txn.keyreg.*`.

### AW-2026-114 — ARC-56 registry container is served on the wallet's own origin from a floating image — **Medium** — New (needs verification)

- **Affected:** `k8s/deployment-main.yaml:141-147`, `k8s/deployment-stable.yaml:142,176`, `k8s/deployment-stable-biatec.yaml:140,174` (ingress path `/arc56-registry/(.*)` on the wallet hosts); `:23-24` of each (`image: scholtz2/arc56-registry:latest`, `imagePullPolicy: Always`); `.github/workflows/awallet-main.yml:114-121`, `awallet-stable.yml:170-176` (restarted on every deploy to pull the newest image); `src/scripts/arc56/registry.ts:20-24` (same-origin path is the first mirror); `docker/default.conf:8-17` (security headers are set by the wallet container only).
- **Description:** AW-2026-041 rates the registry as display-metadata poisoning and AW-2026-056 notes the `:latest` tag. Neither considers that the container shares the wallet's origin. Any response under `/arc56-registry/` that a browser treats as an active document runs as the wallet: it can read IndexedDB and localStorage and script a same-origin wallet window.
- **Attacker precondition:** control over what `scholtz2/arc56-registry:latest` serves (the external ARC56Registry repository, its image build, or the Docker Hub tag) and the ability to have it serve `text/html` or `image/svg+xml`. **Not verified:** which content types the image serves and which headers it sends — the image is not built from this repository.
- **Impact:** if the precondition holds, key theft from every user of the hosted wallet, from a component outside this repository's review, CI and CodeQL.
- **Recommendation:** serve the registry from a separate origin with CORS for the wallet; if it stays same-origin, pin the image by digest and force `Content-Type: application/json`, `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff` and `Content-Security-Policy: default-src 'none'; sandbox` on that ingress path.

### AW-2026-115 — dApp fee ceiling and type allow-list are skipped for `signers: []` entries, which the wallet still signs — **Low** — New (bypass of the AW-2026-101 fix)

- **Affected:** `src/scripts/liquid/guards.ts:319-321` (fee loop `continue`s on `preSigned` or `signers: []` before the own-sender test), `:302-307` (same exemption in the type check), `:205-207` (comment: "`signers: []` is not enforced by the signing path"); `src/components/ConnectRequestsTable.vue:1302-1357, 1484-1502` (`clickSign`/`toBeSigned` never look at `signers`); `src/store/wc.ts:661-676` and the Liquid/Direct/v1 equivalents (return whatever is in `signer.signed`); `playwright/unit/liquidGuards.spec.ts:346-349` asserts the exemption as intended.
- **Description:** a request entry `{ txn, signers: [] }` whose sender is an approved wallet account passes admission with any fee and any type. Probe at HEAD: fee 100 ALGO without `signers` → refused; with `signers: []` → `{"ok":true}`; type `hb` with `signers: []` → `{"ok":true}`. The row then has a normal Sign button and the signature is returned.
- **Attacker precondition:** an approved session on any of the four transports; the user clicks Sign and Send back.
- **Impact:** the per-transaction 0.5 ALGO and per-request 1 ALGO ceilings do not hold. The fee is displayed and the ARC-14 "Authenticate" relabel no longer applies above the minimum fee, so this is the ceiling half of AW-2026-101 only. A pre-signed envelope skips the same checks but is never signed by the wallet.
- **Recommendation:** apply the fee and type checks to every entry whose sender is one of the wallet's accounts, whatever `signers`/`preSigned` say; or carry `signers` into the wrapper and never sign or return those positions (closes AW-2026-096 as well). Correct the unit test.

### AW-2026-116 — Cached signatures survive a failed answer and every transport-side removal of a request — **Low** — New (residual of AW-2026-104)

- **Affected:** `src/components/ConnectRequestsTable.vue:1416-1417, 1442-1443` (`forgetRequestSignatures` runs only after `sendResult`/`cancelRequest` resolves); `src/store/wc.ts:712-719` (request removed in `finally`, error rethrown, forget skipped), `:304-306` (`session_delete` only refreshes sessions; no `session_request_expire` handler); `src/shared/wc.ts:143-146` and `src/store/liquid.ts:199-212, 779-787` (disconnect drops requests without forgetting).
- **Description:** when the answer to the dApp fails, or the dApp/user tears the session down after the user signed but before sending back, the request disappears and the signature stays in `signer.signed` under its txID until logout or a network switch. An identical transaction in a later request from any session shows "Signed" with "Send back" enabled.
- **Attacker precondition:** a dApp that closes its channel after the user signed, then re-submits the same bytes within the validity window.
- **Impact:** as AW-2026-104: same bytes, bounded by `lastValid`, but released without a fresh Sign click.
- **Recommendation:** forget in a `finally`; forget in the session/connector removal paths and on `session_delete`; key the cache by request id plus txID.

### AW-2026-117 — Asset transfers are labelled with the native token (or nothing) when asset metadata is missing — **Low** — New (residual of AW-2026-090)

- **Affected:** `src/components/ConnectRequestsTable.vue:1704-1707, 1727-1731` with `src/main.ts:171-173` (`getAssetName` returns `undefined`, so `formatCurrency`'s default `currency = tokenSymbol` applies; decimals fall back to 0); `src/pages/Sign.vue:143-157, 838-846, 1996-2008` (plain review shows "Algo" and divides by 10^6 unless the indexer lookup succeeded); `src/pages/SignAll.vue:558` (`<Column field="asset">` on a row type with no `asset` field, `:23-30`; no asset-id row in the expansion); `src/store/indexer.ts:399-402` (a failed lookup returns `undefined` silently).
- **Description:** when the indexer is slow, unreachable or lying, an `axfer` of N base units renders as "N Algo" in the dApp request table (the WalletConnect table has a separate asset-id column; the compact Direct line does not), as "N/10^6 Algo" on `/sign`, and with an empty asset column on `/signAll`. The asset id from the transaction itself is not shown on `/sign` or `/signAll`.
- **Attacker precondition:** indexer failure or a malicious indexer (AW-2026-010/-011); for `/signAll`, a scheduler-built group.
- **Impact:** the user reviews the wrong asset and scale. The type row still says `axfer`.
- **Recommendation:** always render `assetTransfer.assetIndex` from the transaction; without metadata show raw base units as `asset <id>`, as the foreign-network branch (`ConnectRequestsTable.vue:1724-1726`) already does.

### AW-2026-118 — Deleting the last account is never persisted; the key returns on the next unlock — **Low** — New

- **Affected:** `src/store/wallet.ts:1819-1824` (`saveWallet` returns `false` when `privateAccounts` is empty: "check not to empty the wallet"), `:1208-1215` (`deleteAccount` ignores the save result and returns `true`); `src/pages/AccountOverview.vue:613-622` (navigates away as on success).
- **Description:** after the only (or last remaining) account is deleted, the encrypted record still holds its `sk`/`hdMnemonic`/`falconMnemonic`. The UI reports success; the account reappears at the next login.
- **Attacker precondition:** none; normal use. Relevant to a user removing a key from a shared or to-be-handed-over device.
- **Impact:** key material the user believes deleted remains on the device (still encrypted with the wallet password).
- **Recommendation:** let an explicit delete write an empty list (keep the guard for the not-yet-loaded case only), or fail the delete visibly.

### AW-2026-119 — Account deletion needs no password and no backup check; deleting an HD root orphans its derived accounts; destroying a wallet leaves other tabs unlocked — **Low** — New (the AW-2026-108 goal is only partly met)

- **Affected:** `src/components/account/AccountOverviewHeader.vue:33-55`, `src/pages/AccountOverview.vue:613-622`, `src/store/wallet.ts:1192-1227` (no `checkPassword`, no `backedUp` check, no check for accounts whose `hdRootAddr` is the deleted address); `src/store/signer.ts:556-563` (`signByHd` throws without the root); `src/store/wallet.ts:1829-1832` (`saveWallet` returns silently when the wallet record is gone), `:2046-2100` (`destroyWallet` resets only the current tab and does not call `wc.clear()`).
- **Description:** the password dialog added for wallet destruction does not cover deleting individual accounts, which is equally irreversible for that key. Deleting an HD root removes the only copy of the mnemonic for all derived accounts, which stay listed but can no longer sign or be exported. After a wallet is destroyed in one tab, another unlocked tab keeps its keys in memory and reports new accounts as created although nothing is stored.
- **Attacker precondition:** physical access to an unlocked session (the case AW-2026-108 addressed), or the user's own mistake.
- **Impact:** loss of keys, not theft.
- **Recommendation:** require the password to delete an account that holds a secret; refuse or warn when `backedUp` is false or HD children reference the root; treat a missing wallet record in `saveWallet` as an error and log other tabs out on destroy.

### AW-2026-120 — Pay QR scanner ignores `network=` — **Low** — New (residual of AW-2026-102)

- **Affected:** `src/pages/Pay.vue:1347-1438` (own parser; the `switch` at `:1374-1395` has no `network` case and `applyLinkNetwork` is never called), versus the link path `:1009`.
- **Description:** a scanned QR issued for another network pre-fills recipient, amount and asset id on the currently selected chain with no prompt, whereas the same text opened as a link is refused unless the user confirms the switch. The scanner also still treats `amount` as whole units (AW-2026-111).
- **Attacker precondition:** user scans a QR for another network.
- **Impact:** payment built on the wrong chain with an asset id interpreted there; the environment row on the form is the only cue.
- **Recommendation:** route scanned text through `parseToAccount`.

### AW-2026-121 — `/gateway` link can be crafted "already paid" and auto-forwards the browser to any https origin — **Low** — New (residual of AW-2026-084/-076)

- **Affected:** `src/pages/Merchant.vue:294-321` (no time or round floor), `:376-385` (10-second countdown), `:346-355, 82-97` (auto-submitted `<form :action="settings.success">`); `src/scripts/merchantGateway.ts:151-174` (with no `amount` the minimum is 0), `:31-45` (`sanitizeRedirectUrl` allows any https host).
- **Description:** a link naming a payee and note prefix that match any past payment shows "payment received" on the wallet's domain and POSTs the browser to the link's `success` URL without a click.
- **Attacker precondition:** victim opens a wallet-origin link.
- **Impact:** phishing pivot from the wallet's domain; no funds or keys involved.
- **Recommendation:** accept only payments confirmed after the page loaded; require a click, with the target host shown, instead of auto-submit.

### AW-2026-122 — Application-call `access` list and `rejectVersion` are signed but never displayed — **Low** — New (residual of AW-2026-082)

- **Affected:** `node_modules/algosdk/dist/types/transaction.d.ts:82-83` (`access`, `rejectVersion`); `src/components/ConnectRequestsTable.vue:616-628, 698-758` (only `foreignApps`, `accounts`, `foreignAssets`, `boxes`); no use of `.access`/`rejectVersion` anywhere in `src/`; `src/scripts/direct/protocol.ts:278-336` (Direct does not refuse them although it signs only what the popup shows in full).
- **Description:** an application call that carries its references in the access list shows none of them, and ARC-56 reference arguments decode against the legacy arrays only. Whether `access` is active on the networks in use was not verified.
- **Attacker precondition:** approved dApp session.
- **Impact:** incomplete review of an application call.
- **Recommendation:** render both fields; refuse them on Direct until they are shown.

### AW-2026-123 — Node-parameter and network checks missing where libraries build or return the transaction — **Low** — New (residual of AW-2026-005/-053/-043)

- **Affected:** `src/pages/Pay.vue:1154-1241` and `src/pages/Arc200/OptIn.vue` (ARC-200 transfer/opt-in built by the generated client from the raw algod client); `src/pages/Account/ScheduledPaymentDetail.vue:346-365, 401-447, 460-520` (generated scheduler clients); `src/scripts/aggregators/validate.ts:18-48` (`assertSwapTransactionSafe` has no genesis check; callers `biatec.ts:238-254`, `folks.ts:139-149`, `deflex.ts:158-164`).
- **Description:** on these paths the suggested parameters are fetched by the library, so `assertParamsMatchNetwork` and the abnormal-fee guard do not run at build time; the genesis mismatch is still caught on `/sign` and `/signAll`, the fee is displayed but not capped. Swap transactions returned by an aggregator API are signed with the raw key without comparing their genesis with the selected network. The library's internal fee behaviour was not traced.
- **Attacker precondition:** malicious configured node, or a compromised aggregator API.
- **Impact:** inflated fee on the ARC-200 paths; a signature valid on another network on the swap path.
- **Recommendation:** pass checked parameters to these builders; run `checkTxGenesis` inside `assertSwapTransactionSafe`.
